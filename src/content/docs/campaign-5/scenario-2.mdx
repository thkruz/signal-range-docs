## What the Correlator Can and Cannot See

The Geolocation console cross-correlates the SENTRY-7 downlink against the SENTRY-9 downlink. It finds a signal only if **both satellites relayed it** - an uplink strong enough to enter SENTRY-7's transponder also enters SENTRY-9's sidelobes, and the time and frequency differences between the two copies are what the fix is built from.

A carrier that arrives at the PA-22 dish directly - from a transmitter on the ground, through the dish's own sidelobes - was never on either satellite. Tune the correlator to it and the answer is **NO CORRELATION - check target frequency and bandwidth**, every time, however carefully it is tuned. That is not a fault. It is the correlator telling you the carrier did not come through the bird.

| Tell | Through the transponder (uplink interference) | Direct at the dish (terrestrial RFI) |
|------|-----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Who hears it | Every station under SENTRY-7, and SENTRY-9's sidelobes | This dish, from this spot |
| Fits the transponder plan | Downlink maps to an uplink inside a TP-1 passband | Maps to an uplink outside every passband, or to nothing |
| Correlator | Locks and captures | NO CORRELATION |
| Cadence | Whatever the operator chose - often a duty cycle | Usually continuous; a site that is simply on |
| Doppler | Follows the inclined bird | None |
| Whose problem | The field team's | Spectrum management's |

The C-band downlink (3.7-4.2 GHz) is shared with terrestrial fixed-wireless systems. A sector or backhaul link with a sidelobe pointed at a receive site produces exactly this: a strong, continuous carrier on one dish and nowhere else.

## Correlator Reference

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Integration window | 12 s | Interferer must be transmitting for at least 70% of it |
| Target frequency | Interferer **uplink**, MHz | Within half the carrier's bandwidth of its center |
| Correlation bandwidth | Match the carrier | Within a factor of two |
| CAPTURE | One 12 s integration | TDOA/FDOA on success; NO CORRELATION otherwise |
| COMPUTE FIX | Solves every capture on the pair | Lat/lon and 95% ellipse as ±major×minor km |

A 36 s on-window fits three 12 s captures if the first one starts at the top of the window. Six captures across two or more cycles put most fixes inside 25 km; twelve across four cycles close to 15 km.
