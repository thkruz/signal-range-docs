## Correlator Reference

The Geolocation console cross-correlates the SENTRY-7 downlink against the SENTRY-9 downlink and produces one TDOA/FDOA measurement per capture. It does not know what it is looking for until you tell it.

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Integration window | 12 s | Interferer must be transmitting for at least 70% of it (8.4 s) |
| Target frequency | Interferer **uplink**, MHz | Must be within half the interferer's bandwidth of its center |
| Correlation bandwidth | Match the interferer | Accepted within a factor of two of the carrier's occupied bandwidth |
| Adjacent collector | SENTRY-9 | Pre-selected |
| CAPTURE | One 12 s integration | Result line reports TDOA (µs) and FDOA (Hz), or NO CORRELATION |
| AUTO | Back-to-back captures | Retries through off-windows; each miss still burns 12 s |
| COMPUTE FIX | Solves all captures on the pair | Reports lat/lon and a 95% error ellipse as ±major×minor km |

Measurement noise on this pair is about 1.5 µs TDOA and 3 Hz FDOA. Six captures will get you inside 25 km most nights. The birds drift between on-windows, and every new window rotates the FDOA line of position - so captures spread across cycles tighten the ellipse faster than captures crammed into one.

<Aside type="caution">
NO CORRELATION - interferer not transmitting during integration means you started the capture too late in the on-window or during the off-window. It is not a fault. Time the cadence before you touch CAPTURE.
</Aside>
