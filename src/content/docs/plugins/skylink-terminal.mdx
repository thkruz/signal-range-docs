SkyLink is the app that comes with a consumer satellite terminal. The plugin adds it as a station
tab named **SkyLink** on field stations, such as the posts in the Frontline campaign. It shows
what the dish says about itself: status, speeds, outages and a sky map. It does not show the real
cause of a fault. Use it as one clue and confirm with your own measurements.

![The SkyLink app home screen on a field station.](/images/shots/plugins/skylink-terminal.webp)

## When the app is available

The app talks to the dish over the router. What you see depends on the link between them.

| What you see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Cannot connect** | The router is off, still booting, or the laptop is not on it. The app shows nothing else. |
| **Can't reach your SkyLink** | The router is fine but the dish is off, still booting, or the cable is cut. You see the last data it sent, with a "Last updated" time. |
| Normal screens | The dish answers. |

## Screens

Use the **SkyLink menu** to move between screens.

### Home

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | One word: Online, Searching, Booting, Offline or Sleeping. |
| **Alerts** | Active alerts, or "No active alerts." |
| **Download**, **Upload** | Current speed in Mbps. |
| **Latency** | Round trip in ms. |
| **Uptime** | Time since the last start. |

### Obstructions

A sky map with north at the top. It fills in over time as the dish follows satellites.

*   **Clear sky**, **Blocked sky** and **Not seen yet** are the three cell colours.
*   **Blocked** and **Sky seen** give the percentages.
*   **Check for obstructions** starts a 15 minute check. Let time run. **Stop check** ends it early.
*   **Reset map** deletes the map after you confirm. The dish starts a new one.

### Statistics

Pick a **Time range** of 15 minutes, 6 hours or 12 hours. The app plots **Download**, **Upload**,
**Latency** and **Ping drop**. An **Outages** table lists each outage with **Started**,
**Duration** and **Cause**.

### Speed test

**Start speed test** runs for about 25 seconds. The result shows **Download**, **Upload**,
**Latency** and **Limited by**. Limited by reads SkyLink when the dish set the speed, or Local
network when the router Wi-Fi did. Earlier tests are listed below.

### Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Restart SkyLink** | Restarts the dish after you confirm. No service until it has started, which can take several minutes. |
| **Snow melt** | **Automatic** heats only when snow is on the dish. **Pre-heat** also heats when it is cold. **Off** never heats. The heater uses extra power. |
| **Power save** | **Use a sleep schedule** with **Sleep from** and **Wake at** times. The dish has no service while it sleeps. |
| **Wi-Fi** | Network name and a password of at least 8 characters. |

Settings are not available while the dish cannot be reached.

### Advanced

Read-only debug data for support: **Software version**, **Uptime**, **Boot count**, **Location**
(Valid or Not valid), **GNSS satellites**, **Active alerts**, **Tilt / boresight** and
**Obstruction**. **Copy debug data** puts it on the clipboard.

## Common tasks

### Check whether the dish is blocked

<Steps>

1. Open **Obstructions**.
2. Press **Check for obstructions** and let time run for the 15 minutes shown.
3. Read **Blocked**. Blocked cells show where trees, a net or a building cut the view.

</Steps>

### Tell a slow dish from a slow network

<Steps>

1. Open **Speed test** and press **Start speed test**.
2. Wait for the result.
3. Read **Limited by**. Local network means the router, not the dish, is the limit.

</Steps>

### Read the outage history

<Steps>

1. Open **Statistics** and choose a **Time range**.
2. Read the **Outages** table.
3. Compare the **Cause** with what you measure at the station.

</Steps>

## Status and what it means

The cause labels are deliberately vague. Several real faults share one label.

| Label | What the dish could observe | Real causes that look like this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Obstructed** | A blocked field of view. | Snow, a net, a tree line. |
| **Searching** | Dish is up but not locked. | A normal beam handover, no position fix, a link that will not register. |
| **Booting** | The dish restarted. | A cut cable, a power sag, a restart by the user, a power loss. |
| **Thermal shutdown** | The dish shut itself down hot. | Overheating. |
| **Offline** | The network refuses the terminal. | Account or provider problem. Shown with "Network unavailable". |

Alerts: **Obstructed**, **Thermal throttle**, **Thermal shutdown** and **Heating**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cannot connect | Router off or laptop not on it. | Power the router and join the laptop to it. |
| Can't reach your SkyLink | Dish off, booting, or cable path broken. | Check power and the dish cable. Wait out the boot. |
| Searching for a long time | No position fix, or no registration. | Check **Location** and **GNSS satellites** on **Advanced**. |
| Booting repeats | A cable or supply fault. | Inspect the cable and connectors, then the supply. |
| Map is empty | It needs time to fill. | Let time run. |
| Settings unavailable | The dish cannot be reached. | Restore the link first. |
