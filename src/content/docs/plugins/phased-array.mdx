The phased array plugin adds a Starlink-style Ku-band flat panel to SignalRange. It has no dish to
point. It steers its beam electronically, follows one Starlink satellite at a time and hands over
to the next one before the first sets. Its station tab is **Array Control**, and it replaces the
ACU Control tab that dish antennas use.

The plugin is built in and on by default. You meet it in the Range Watch sandbox called Starlink
Interference, where the Cedar Mesa station (CM-01) carries the panel.

![Array Control page with the sky view, beam card, GEO protection zone and handover strip.](/images/shots/plugins/phased-array.webp)

## How the panel behaves

*   The panel looks straight up. The beam can lean up to 57 degrees away from straight up. Anything
    beyond that is outside the scan cone and cannot be used.
*   Leaning costs gain. The farther the beam leans, the higher the **Scan loss**.
*   The panel serves Starlink satellites only. A geostationary satellite is never selected, even if
    it sits in view.
*   Every handover, and the first acquisition, drops the link for about 100 ms.
*   In AUTO the panel keeps the current satellite until another one is more than 8 degrees higher
    in the sky. Then it hands over.
*   The receive band is 10.7 to 12.7 GHz and the transmit band is 14.0 to 14.5 GHz.
*   Gain and G/T are game values for a Ku flat panel. They are not measured Starlink figures.

## Controls and readouts

| Control | What it does | Typical value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AUTO** | The panel picks a satellite and hands over by itself. | Default |
| **MANUAL** | The beam holds the satellite you lock and never hands over. | Used for tests |
| **STOW** | Parks the beam on the panel normal, straight up. No link. | Varies |
| Sky view | Click a dot to lock that satellite. Shift-click or right-click pins it. | n/a |
| **Keep the beam out of the GEO arc** | Switch for the GEO protection zone. | On |
| **BAND HALF-WIDTH** | Distance the beam must stay clear of the GEO arc. Buttons step by 5 or 1. Range 0 to 30. | 8 DEG |
| **APPLY** / **CANCEL** | Apply or discard a staged band width. | n/a |

Readouts on the **Beam** card:

| Readout | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Target** | Satellite name and NORAD number. Reads No target or Stowed when nothing is tracked. |
| **Beam az / el** | Where the beam points now. |
| **Scan angle** | Angle from the panel normal, with the limit in brackets. |
| **Beamwidth now** | Beam width at the current scan angle. It widens as the beam leans. |
| **G/T now** | Receive figure of merit at the current scan angle. |
| **Scan loss** | Gain lost to leaning, in dB, with a bar that fills toward the limit. |
| **Last link drop** | UTC time of the last drop and the satellite that was serving. |

Sky view dots:

*   **visible** satellites can be locked.
*   **outside scan cone** satellites cannot, or sit inside the protection band.
*   **target** is the satellite being tracked.
*   **next handover** marks the satellite the panel will move to.
*   The shaded **GEO band** shows the protection zone. It stays drawn when the zone is off.

## Common tasks

### Watch a handover

<Steps>

1. Open the **Array Control** tab and make sure **AUTO** is lit.
2. Look at the **Handovers, next 10 minutes** strip. The red line is now. Each block is one satellite.
3. Read the note under the strip. It gives the time to the next handover and the reason.
4. When the time comes, the status badge shows HANDOVER OUTAGE for a moment. Any modem fed by the
   panel loses lock at the same time.

</Steps>

Skip time forward if you do not want to wait.

### Lock a satellite by hand

<Steps>

1. Press **MANUAL**.
2. Click a dot on the sky view.
3. Check **Scan angle** and **Scan loss**. A satellite low in the sky gives a high loss.
4. If a message says the lock was refused, see the refusal table below.

</Steps>

A manual lock is dropped if the satellite sets or leaves the scan cone. A notice then says why.

### Test the GEO protection zone

<Steps>

1. Switch off **Keep the beam out of the GEO arc**.
2. In **MANUAL**, lock a Starlink whose beam lies inside the GEO arc.
3. Watch the red warning and the **GEO interference** badge.
4. Switch the protection back on.

</Steps>

### Change the band width

<Steps>

1. Type a value or use the step buttons. The **APPLY** button becomes active.
2. Press **APPLY**. **CANCEL** puts the old value back.

</Steps>

## Status and what it means

Top badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| LINK UP (green) | The beam is on a satellite. |
| HANDOVER OUTAGE (amber) | A handover is in progress. About 100 ms. |
| NO LINK (red) | No satellite is tracked. |

**GEO interference** badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| CLEAR (green) | Beam is well away from the arc, or there is no target. |
| NEAR GEO ARC (amber) | Beam is within twice the band width of the arc. |
| IN GEO ARC (red) | Beam points inside the band. A red warning also shows. |
| MAIN BEAM ON ARC (red) | The arc sits within 3 dB of the beam peak. |

The card shows geometry and the antenna pattern only. The terminal has no transmit power model
here, so it does not report EIRP.

Lock refusals:

| Message | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| Lock refused: that satellite is not tracked. | The satellite is not in the Starlink set the panel serves. |
| Lock refused: the satellite is below the horizon. | It is not up yet. |
| Lock refused: outside the panel scan limit. | More than 57 degrees from straight up. |
| Lock refused: inside the GEO protection band. | Protection is on and the satellite lies in the band. |

Handover strip labels: SL-nnnn is a Starlink, **no coverage** is a gap with nothing to hand over
to, and **not predicted** is the part of the 10 minutes the prediction does not reach yet.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Few or no dots | Few Starlinks are above the horizon or inside the scan cone. | Skip time forward. |
| Link keeps dropping | Each handover costs about 100 ms. | This is normal. |
| Cannot lock a GEO satellite | The panel serves Starlink only. | Use a dish antenna for GEO work. |
| No handovers listed in the strip | The terminal is in MANUAL or STOW. | Press **AUTO**. |
| Band width will not change | The change is staged. | Press **APPLY**. |
