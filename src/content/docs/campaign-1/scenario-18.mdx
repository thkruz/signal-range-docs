## Tracking Strategy (SOP-TK-002)

Program-track follows the ephemeris. When the ephemeris is wrong, program-track
points at where the satellite *should* be. Step-track follows the beacon - where
the satellite actually *is*.

### Transition Checklist

- [ ] Confirm beacon C/N degradation under program-track (document the baseline first)
- [ ] Enable step-track optimization (requires program-track engaged underneath)
- [ ] Confirm beacon lock recovers and holds
- [ ] Verify carrier C/N stabilizes at or near baseline
- [ ] Hold and monitor - the drift grows slowly; step-track keeps up

<Aside type="caution">
Do not chase the bird manually. Manual pointing against a growing figure-eight
is a losing game and risks driving the dish off the beacon entirely. Step-track
exists precisely for a satellite whose ephemeris cannot be trusted.
</Aside>

Escalate to Dana if: beacon lock cannot be held in step-track, C/N trends below
demod threshold despite good tracking, or Halifax declares the vehicle unsafe to
operate. Otherwise: keep the link, feed observations to Halifax, log everything
with timestamps - your ground-side track history is data the vehicle team uses.
