### Expected Complications

Real first light operations include complications. Handle them professionally:

**Complication 1: Weak Beacon Signal (Will occur at beacon acquisition)**
- Expected: -95 dBm
- Actual: -98 dBm (3 dB weaker)
- Cause: Spacecraft team's prediction slightly off (common)
- Solution: Increase LNB gain to compensate
- Not a failure: This is normal operations

**Complication 2: Slow Antenna Slew (Will occur during pointing)**
- Specified rate: 1.0°/second
- Actual rate: 0.75°/second (25% slower)
- Cause: Antenna performance degradation (age/maintenance)
- Effect: Takes longer to reach position (~8 minutes vs 5 minutes)
- Solution: Wait for completion, don't rush
- Not a fault: Equipment within acceptable limits

He's watching how you handle these. Real operators encounter variations from specs regularly. The question is: Do you adapt appropriately?
