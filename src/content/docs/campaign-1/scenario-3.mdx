## Weather Briefing

**Current Conditions (1045):**
- Temperature: 26°F (-3°C)
- Wind: NW at 25 mph, gusts to 40
- Precipitation: Heavy snow, 2+ inches/hour
- Visibility: 0.25 miles

**Forecast (next 6 hours):**
- Blizzard conditions continuing through 1700
- Additional accumulation: 12-18 inches
- Wind-driven snow creating significant signal attenuation

**Link Impact:**
- Clear-sky C/N on the TIDEMARK-1 carrier: about 12.7 dB. The modem holds lock down to about 4.2 dB (QPSK 3/4), so Vermont starts with roughly 8 dB of margin.
- At C-band, falling snow itself costs well under 1 dB. The damage comes from snow and ice loading the feed: every dB of ice on the feed takes about 3 dB off C/N, because the wet feed both absorbs the carrier and radiates its own noise into the LNB. About 3 dB of ice on an unheated feed is enough to lose lock.
- Projected at storm peak: Vermont cannot hold the 4.2 dB threshold. Plan to be off VT-01 before then.

<Aside type="caution">
Don't wait for the AGC to warn you. The AGC card shows its **Gain** (dB), its **Input** level (dBm) and its **Output** level (dBm). VT-01's AGC holds the output at -30 dBm and has a 10 dB maximum gain. It levels the total power it sees, carrier plus noise, so as the feed loads up the input level drops only a dB or two and the gain rises by the same amount. At VT-01 the gain reads about -2.5 dB in clear sky and is still under +1 dB when the carrier loses lock; the card never gets near **At Max**. Watch the carrier's C/N on RX Analysis: that is the number that tells you how much time you have.
</Aside>

## Weather Handover Procedure (SOP-WX-003)

Operations rolled out this procedure last month after the January ice storm revealed gaps in our ad-hoc approach. This is the first time VT-01 has needed to execute it.

<Aside type="caution">
Maine's look angles differ from Vermont's. Program Track calculates correct pointing automatically - never manually enter Vermont's coordinates at Maine.
</Aside>

### Pre-Handover: Protect Vermont

- [ ] Enable feed heater on Vermont ACU
- [ ] Note the AGC Gain (dB) and Input (dBm), and the carrier's C/N

### Backup Site Activation (Maine)

**Timing Verification**
- [ ] GPSDO locked

**Antenna**
- [ ] Tracking mode: Program Track (PROGRAM)

### Handover Log Entry

Type the handover into the Ops Log (station sidebar) before you sign off. Every weather handover gets an entry, even a clean one:

## AGC Reference

What the VT-01 AGC card shows (output target -30 dBm, maximum gain +10 dB):

| Readout | Clear sky | Feed loaded, near loss of lock | What it tells you |
|---------|-----------|--------------------------------|-------------------|
| Gain | about -2.5 dB | about +0.5 dB | How hard the AGC is working. A few dB of movement is normal. |
| Input | about -27.5 dBm | about -30.5 dBm | Total power arriving at the AGC: carrier plus noise |
| Output | -30.0 dBm | -30.0 dBm | Held at target; a steady output says nothing about link health |
| Mode | Active | Active | **At Max** means the gain has reached +10 dB: there is almost no carrier left to level (pre-acquisition, a dead LNB) |

The AGC keeps the level steady for the modem. It cannot restore C/N: it turns the noise up and down with the carrier. Judge the link by C/N and lock margin, not by the AGC.
