## Morning Round Sequence (SOP-OPS-001)

### VT-01 / TIDEMARK-1

- [ ] Dashboard: no warning or critical alarms
- [ ] GPSDO: locked, stable reference
- [ ] RX: TM-1 beacon present at 1,074.5 MHz IF, receiver locked, healthy C/N (about 13 dB on our own TP-1 carrier); note the reading
- [ ] TX: HPA enabled, standard 10 dB backoff (about 50 dBm, 100 W out), no overdrive

### ME-02 / TIDEMARK-2

- [ ] Dashboard: no warning or critical alarms
- [ ] RX: LNB powered, noise temperature normal; retune the analyzer to 1,070 MHz IF (it may still be parked on another bird's beacon) and confirm the TM-2 beacon
- [ ] Antenna: locked on TIDEMARK-2 in program-track
- [ ] Payload spot-check on RX Analysis > Payload Data Integrity: frame sync, CRC, FEC indicators (the Dashboard does not show them) (ME-02's receiver hears its own TM-2 hub carrier, relayed by the bird)

### TIDEMARK-3 Spot-Check (from VT-01)

- [ ] Sister teleport confirms it has TM-1 customer traffic (already coordinated)
- [ ] TX inhibit: disable VT-01's HPA output before the dish moves
- [ ] Repoint VT-01 to TIDEMARK-3 in program-track (Az 145.7°, El 32.7°)
- [ ] Spectrum analyzer to 1,078 MHz IF - beacon present and clean
- [ ] Return VT-01 to TIDEMARK-1, retune analyzer to 1,074.5 MHz IF
- [ ] Take TM-1 back: enable VT-01's HPA output once the dish is locked on TIDEMARK-1
- [ ] Final alarm sweep, then type the round summary into the Operations Log (rounds complete, TM-3 beacon verified)

<Aside type="note">
The TM-3 check is receive-only. No transmit configuration is required - the only question this morning is whether the bird's beacon is where the commissioning crew says it is, seen independently from our own antenna.

Never slew a keyed dish. With VT-01's HPA on, the dish sweeping off TIDEMARK-1 would paint every satellite along the arc with 100 W, and while the sister teleport carries TM-1 a second VT-01 carrier on the same frequency would interfere with it. Inhibit TX first, move, and bring the HPA back only once you are locked on TIDEMARK-1 again. While TX is inhibited VT-01's Dashboard shows two alarms: "AGC on noise only" (with our carrier off, the receive modem's passband holds noise and no traffic carrier) and "Carrier lock lost" (Modem 1 has no carrier to lock to). Both are expected, both clear once VT-01 takes TM-1 back, and both belong in the shift log as raised and cleared.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
TM-1 customer traffic rides on the sister teleport only while VT-01 is off-pointed. Do not begin the TM-3 spot-check until the round on TM-1 is complete, and bring VT-01 back to TIDEMARK-1 promptly when the beacon check is done.
</Aside>

## Why the Beacon Check Matters

A clean CW spike at the expected IF confirms two things at once: the antenna is pointed at the right bird, and the LNB LO is converting where it should. Wrong pointing produces no signal; wrong LO puts the signal at the wrong IF. One observation, two verifications.
