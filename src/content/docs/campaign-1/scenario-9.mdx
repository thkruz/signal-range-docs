## Why the Beacon Check Matters

A clean CW spike at the expected IF confirms two things at once: the antenna is pointed at the right bird, and the LNB LO is converting where it should. Wrong pointing produces no signal; wrong LO puts the signal at the wrong IF. One observation, two verifications.

### Handle Yourself

- Routine round checks and analyzer retuning
- VT-01 repoint to TM-3 and back (pre-coordinated)
- Log entries

### Inform Dana

- Any active alarm on either station
- TM-3 beacon missing, off-frequency, or degraded
- Sister teleport reports any TM-1 customer impact during the spot-check window

A missing TM-3 beacon is not yours to troubleshoot this morning - it goes back to the commissioning crew through Dana. Your job is the independent observation, not the repair.
