## Escalation Guidelines

You hold command. Hand a track to Dana only when it needs authority you do not
have (SLA concessions, vendor emergency call-out, abandoning a booked pass
without customer agreement). Handing over a track is a decision, not a failure -
but it goes in the log with a reason, like everything else today.
