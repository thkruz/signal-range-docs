<Aside type="caution">
**Backoff discipline.** A 6 dB backoff is acceptable for a 30-minute scheduled window. It is not the right resting state. Returning the amp to 10 dB between passes protects the amplifier and matches the configuration the next operator expects to inherit. Forgetting this step is a paper cut that compounds across shifts.
</Aside>

## EIRP vs Linearity (Reference)

Lowering HPA backoff moves the amplifier closer to saturation. The tradeoff:

| Backoff | EIRP | IMD | Linearity |
|---------|------|-----|-----------|
| 10 dB (resting) | Conservative | Low | Excellent |
| 6 dB (pass window) | +4 dB | Modestly elevated | Good - within spec |
| 3 dB (aggressive) | +7 dB | High | Marginal - overdrive risk |
| 1 dB (saturation) | Max | Very high | Compressed - **do not run here** |

The 6 dB target for the SeaLink window is the standard high-throughput backoff for AURORA-7. Watch the overdrive indicator across the pass; if it ever trips, raise backoff before doing anything else.
