## The Assignment

**Deliverable:** AURORA-7 Quick-Reference Card (in-game Working Document)

**Method:** Perform the live procedure on AURORA-7. At each phase, a knowledge
check asks which callout, formula, or warning belongs on the card. Correct
choices are appended to the document automatically.

**Audience:** A trained-but-green operator on their first solo shift. They know
what an LNB is. They do not yet know which mistakes this station's history says
they will make.

<Aside type="note">
A quick-reference card is not a procedure. The procedure lives in the SOP. The
card is what someone tapes to the console: the numbers they will need under
pressure, and the three mistakes that have actually happened here. Selecting
what to leave OFF the card is most of the work.
</Aside>

## AURORA-7 Reference (the procedure being taught)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Orbit | Inclined GEO (~3°), figure-8 drift, Az 190° ± 3°, El 32° ± 3° |
| Beacon RF | 4,165 MHz (V-pol, CW, weak - aging bird) |
| Beacon IF (LNB LO 5,250) | 1,085 MHz |
| Carrier (Downlink) | 3,828 MHz -> 1,422 MHz IF |
| Carrier (Uplink) | 6,053 MHz; BUC LO 7,500 -> 1,447 MHz IF |
| Bandwidth | 24 MHz (narrower than TIDEMARK - legacy) |
| Required tracking | Program-track to acquire, step-track to hold |

## Station History (instructor notes - candidate "common mistakes")

These have all actually happened at VT-01. Some belong on the card; some are
too rare to spend card space on. Your call.

- New operator engaged step-track from MANUAL mode; step-track rides program-track and the dish wandered off the bird.
- Beacon "missing" at 1,085 MHz - LNB LO was set to 6,080 (Maine default) instead of 5,250 after a cross-site shift.
- Operator chased the figure-8 with manual nudges for twenty minutes instead of trusting step-track.
- Spectrum span left at 2 kHz from a beacon check; operator concluded the 24 MHz carrier was "gone."
- TX IF computed against BUC LO 7,000 instead of AURORA-7's 7,500 - carrier landed 500 MHz off.

Anyone can execute a procedure. Teaching it means knowing which three of the
fifty things you do are the ones a new operator will get wrong.

Fly it clean, choose well, and hand Dana a card you would have wanted on your
