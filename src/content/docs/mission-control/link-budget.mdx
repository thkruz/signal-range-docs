The Link Analysis tab is a worksheet for planning a satellite link and then proving it. You enter the numbers for the downlink, optionally the uplink, and compute the carrier-to-noise ratio (C/N). Then you compare that prediction with the C/N the modem measures, and accept the link if the margin is good enough.

This page covers the tab. The formulas and the reasoning behind each term are in [Link Budget](/systems/link-budget/).

The tab appears only in scenarios that ask for a link check, and the heading shows the name of the link being planned.

## Controls

### Downlink Budget fields

| Control | What it does | Typical value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Satellite EIRP (dBm) | Power the satellite radiates toward you, including antenna gain. | varies |
| Free-space path loss (dB) | Loss from distance between satellite and station. | varies |
| RX antenna gain (dBi) | Gain of your receiving dish. | varies |
| System noise temp (K) | Total noise temperature of the receive chain. Must be at least 1. | varies |
| Occupied bandwidth (MHz) | Width of the signal. Must be above 0. | varies |
| Implementation loss (dB) | Extra losses such as cabling or modem imperfection. Starts at 0. | 0 |
| Compute C/N | Calculates the predicted C/N from the fields above. | not applicable |

### Uplink Budget fields

Leave this panel blank for a direct downlink. Fill it in for a transponded link, where the signal is relayed through the satellite. The composite C/N is the one that is graded.

| Control | What it does | Typical value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uplink EIRP (dBW) | Power your station radiates toward the satellite, including antenna gain. | varies |
| Uplink path loss (dB) | Loss on the path up to the satellite. | varies |
| Satellite G/T (dB/K) | Receive quality of the satellite. | varies |
| C/IM (optional) (dB) | Optional intermodulation term. | blank |

The panel shows C/N uplink, C/N downlink and Composite C/N once you press Compute C/N.

<Aside type="note">
Uplink power in the simulator is EIRP, the radiated power after the antenna. Enter the value your station radiates, not the amplifier output.
</Aside>

### Link Verification fields

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Measured C/N (active modem) | Live C/N from the modem. |
| Demod threshold | Lowest C/N at which the demodulator works. |
| Required margin | How far above the threshold the link must be. The default is 3 dB. |
| Live margin | Measured C/N minus the demod threshold. |
| Accept Link | Locks in the live margin. Disabled until the modem gives a measurement. |
| Accepted margin | The margin you locked in. |

## How it works

### Panels

The tab has three panels.

- **Downlink Budget - Predicted:** your calculation for the satellite-to-ground path.
- **Uplink Budget - Predicted:** optional, for links that go through a transponder.
- **Link Verification - Measured:** what the simulator measures, and the Accept Link button.

## Common tasks

### Complete the worksheet

<Steps>

1. Collect the station and satellite values you need from your briefing and the equipment pages.
2. Enter all six downlink fields. Every one is required.
3. If the link goes through a transponder, enter Uplink EIRP, Uplink path loss and Satellite G/T. Leave all three blank otherwise. C/IM may stay blank.
4. Press Compute C/N.
5. Read Predicted C/N and the badge beside it.
6. Compare with the measured value on the right. If they differ, find which input is wrong and recompute.

</Steps>

### Accept the link

<Steps>

1. Make sure the modem is locked on the carrier so Measured C/N shows a number.
2. Check Live margin is at or above Required margin.
3. Press Accept Link.
4. Read the badge under Accepted margin.

</Steps>

## Status and alarms

### Badges and what they mean

| Badge | Where | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NO SOLUTION | Next to Predicted C/N | No worksheet has been computed yet. |
| IN FAMILY | Next to Predicted C/N | Your predicted C/N is within tolerance of the simulator's own clear-sky value. The default tolerance is 1 dB. |
| OUT OF FAMILY | Next to Predicted C/N | Your prediction is too far from the simulator's value. An input is probably wrong. |
| PENDING | Under Accepted margin | You have not accepted the link. |
| LINK GO | Under Accepted margin | The accepted margin meets the required margin. |
| NO-GO | Under Accepted margin | The accepted margin is below the required margin. |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Budget incomplete — all fields required." | A downlink field is blank. | Fill every downlink field. Implementation loss may be 0. |
| "Uplink budget incomplete — fill in EIRP, path loss and G/T, or leave all three blank." | Only some of the three required uplink fields are filled. | Fill Uplink EIRP, path loss and G/T, or clear all three. |
| OUT OF FAMILY. | A wrong input, often path loss, bandwidth units or EIRP units. | Recheck each field. EIRP is in dBm on the downlink and dBW on the uplink. |
| Accept Link is greyed out. | No measured C/N. | Lock the modem on the carrier and check the antenna is pointed. |
| NO-GO after accepting. | Margin below the requirement. | Improve the link (pointing, rain fade, interference), then accept again. |
| Measured C/N is far below prediction. | Weather, interference, mispointing or a fault. | See [Weather and Rain Fade](/systems/weather-and-rain-fade/) and [Interference and Electronic Attack](/systems/interference-and-ea/). |
