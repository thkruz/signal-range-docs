Mission Overview is the first page of a mission. It lays out a card for every ground station and every satellite in the scenario, with a live status on each. Click a card to open that asset. Use it to check the whole picture at the start of a shift and to see at a glance which station needs attention.

![Mission Overview with ground station cards above and satellite cards below.](/images/shots/mission-control/mission-overview.webp)

## What each card shows

### Ground Stations

| Field | What it shows | Typical value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name and ID | The station's name and short identifier. | varies |
| Status badge | OFFLINE, MAINTENANCE, STOWED, DEGRADED or OPERATIONAL. Updates live. | OPERATIONAL |
| Latitude, Longitude | Where the station is. | varies |
| ANT, RF-FE, TX MODEMS, RX MODEMS | Counts of antennas, RF front ends, transmit modems and receive modems. | varies |

### Satellites

| Field | What it shows | Typical value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name and NORAD ID | The satellite and its catalog number. | varies |
| Health badge | Health as a percentage. Green at 90 percent or more, yellow below. | 100% |
| Azimuth, Elevation | Where the satellite is in the sky. A caption says which station the angles are from. | varies |
| TRANSPONDERS | Transponder count and how many are active. | varies |

The look angles on satellite cards follow the last station you selected. Before you select one they use the first station in the scenario.

<Aside type="note">
In scenarios with a large constellation, only the satellites the scenario authors and the ones you pin get a card. To add one, search the constellation in the asset tree and pin it. Pinned satellites then appear here.
</Aside>

## Common tasks

- **Open a station.** Click its card. The tab bar changes to that station's tabs.
- **Open a satellite.** Click its card to open its [Satellite Dashboard](/mission-control/satellite-dashboard/).
- **Spot a problem fast.** Scan the status badges. Anything other than OPERATIONAL or a healthy green badge needs a look.

## Status and what it means

| Station status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| OPERATIONAL | The station is up and no error alarm is active on it. |
| DEGRADED | An error-level alarm is active on the station. Open the Dashboard tab to see which. |
| STOWED | The antenna is stowed. |
| MAINTENANCE | The antenna is parked in maintenance mode. |
| OFFLINE | The station is not operational. |

A lost carrier at the end of a pass does not make a station DEGRADED. The satellite has simply set.

## Troubleshooting

- **A station shows DEGRADED.** Open its Dashboard tab and read the active alarms.
- **A station shows STOWED when you expected tracking.** Open its ACU Control tab and switch the antenna out of stow.
- **A satellite is missing from the cards.** In a constellation scenario, pin it from the asset tree.
- **Look angles are for the wrong station.** Select the station you want in the asset tree. The cards update to its view.
