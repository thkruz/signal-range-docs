The GPS Timing tab is the front panel of the GPS-disciplined oscillator (GPSDO). The GPSDO gives the station a 10 MHz reference and accurate time. The converters in the receive and transmit chains lock to that reference. If the GPSDO drifts or loses GNSS, those converters inherit the error.

![GPS Timing tab with lock, GNSS constellation and holdover cards.](/images/shots/mission-control/gps-timing.webp)

## Controls

| Control | What it does | Typical value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power | Turns the GPSDO on or off. After power-on it warms up before it can lock. | On |
| GNSS Input | Connects or disconnects the GNSS antenna feed. Off simulates losing the signal. | On |

## Status

| Card | Readout | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lock & Power Status | Lock Status | LOCKED, ACQUIRING, UNLOCKED or OFF. |
| Lock & Power Status | GNSS Signal | "(n) SATS" when healthy, WEAK with fewer than 4 satellites, NO SIGNAL, or OFF. |
| Lock & Power Status | Warmup | READY, a countdown in minutes and seconds, or OFF. |
| Lock & Power Status | Holdover | ACTIVE or INACTIVE. |
| GNSS Constellation | Satellites Tracked, Constellation, UTC Accuracy | What the GNSS receiver sees and how close it is to UTC, in ns. |
| GNSS Constellation | GNSS vs REF ΔT | The GNSS time solution minus the disciplined reference, in microseconds. |
| OCXO Oven Control | Oven Temperature, Operating Hours | The oven that keeps the crystal stable. |
| 10 MHz Distribution | Active Outputs, output list | How many 10 MHz outputs are in use out of those available. |
| Reference Quality Metrics | Frequency Accuracy, Allan Deviation (1s), Phase Noise @ 10Hz, Lock Duration | How good the reference is now. |
| Holdover Performance | Status, Duration, Error, Time to Limit | How the reference is coasting without GNSS. |

## Lock, holdover and what they mean

- **Warm-up.** The oven must reach temperature first. The Warmup badge counts down. The GPSDO cannot lock until it reads READY.
- **Locked.** The oscillator is disciplined to GNSS. Downstream converters show Locked.
- **Holdover.** When GNSS goes away, the GPSDO keeps going on its crystal. The error grows with time. The Holdover Performance card shows the error against a 40 microsecond limit, and Time to Limit estimates how long is left. If it passes the limit, Time to Limit reads EXCEEDED.
- **GNSS vs REF ΔT.** With a healthy constellation, this number should stay near zero. If it walks while the constellation looks healthy, the GNSS signal is being spoofed, not lost.

<Aside type="caution">
A walking ΔT with a full satellite count is a spoofing sign, not an outage. Switching GNSS Input off will not cure it, because the oscillator would then coast in holdover.
</Aside>

## Common tasks

### Bring the GPSDO up from cold

<Steps>
1. Turn Power on.
2. Wait for Warmup to count down to READY.
3. Check GNSS Input is on and that GNSS Signal shows a satellite count of 4 or more.
4. Confirm Lock Status reads LOCKED.
</Steps>

### Test a GNSS outage

<Steps>
1. Turn GNSS Input off. GNSS Signal reads NO SIGNAL and Holdover turns ACTIVE.
2. Watch Error and Time to Limit on the Holdover Performance card.
3. Turn GNSS Input on again. The GPSDO re-acquires and Lock Status returns to LOCKED.
</Steps>

## Alarms and what they mean

| Alarm | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| GPSDO not locked | The reference is free-running. Only shown after warm-up ends. Downstream converters will lose lock. |
| GNSS signal lost | No GNSS signal. The unit is in holdover or unlocked. |
| GPSDO in holdover (x.x μs error) | The GPSDO is coasting. Restore GNSS before the error reaches its limit. |
| GPSDO holdover approaching limit (>30 μs) | The holdover error is close to the limit. |
| GPSDO oven temperature out of range (x.x °C) | The crystal oven is outside 65 to 75 °C. |
| GPSDO self-test failed | The internal check has failed. |

The GPSDO light on the Dashboard turns amber or red for these. It stays grey while the unit is off or warming up.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lock Status stays UNLOCKED | Still warming up, GNSS Input is off, or there is no signal. | Wait for READY, turn on GNSS Input, check GNSS Signal. |
| GNSS Signal reads WEAK | Fewer than 4 satellites tracked. | Wait, or check the station for a fault or interference. |
| BUC or LNB Lock lost after a GPSDO fault | They take their 10 MHz from this unit. | Restore GPSDO lock. |
| Time to Limit reads EXCEEDED | Holdover has run past its limit. | Restore GNSS and let the unit re-lock. |
| ΔT walking with full satellites | Suspected GNSS spoofing. | Report it according to the scenario brief. |
