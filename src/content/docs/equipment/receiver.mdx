The Receiver is a multi-modem case that demodulates IF signals from the downlink chain. Each case holds four independent modems, so you can receive several carriers at once. It sits at the end of the receive chain, after the antenna, LNB, IF filter, notch filter and AGC. It is the final decoder: it pulls the data, voice or video out of the modulated carrier.

![Receiver Modems card on the RX Analysis tab with modem buttons, configuration fields and signal-quality readouts.](/images/shots/equipment/receiver.webp)

## Where to find it

The **Receiver Modems** card is on the **RX Analysis** tab of Mission Control, below the Tap Points card. The I&Q Constellation card above it draws the signal on the selected modem. See [RX Analysis](/mission-control/rx-analysis/).

## Controls

Select a modem with the buttons **RX 1** to **RX 4**. The controls apply to the selected modem.

| Control | What it does | Typical value |
|---|---|---|
| Antenna | Chooses which antenna's downlink the modem listens to. | 1 |
| Frequency (MHz) | The IF center frequency to tune. The modem accepts 950 to 2150 MHz. | varies |
| Bandwidth (MHz) | The width the modem captures, 0.1 to 72 MHz. It must be at least as wide as the carrier. | varies |
| Modulation | BPSK, QPSK, 8QAM or 16QAM. It must match the transmitter. | varies |
| FEC Rate | 1/2, 2/3, 3/4, 5/6 or 7/8. It must match the transmitter. | varies |
| Apply Changes | Commits the staged values to the modem. | n/a |
| Power | Powers the selected modem. Power-on takes about 4 seconds. | On |

The fields stage your changes. The "Current" label beside each field shows what the modem is using now. Nothing takes effect until you press **Apply Changes**.

The modem buttons change color to show state: a signal was found, degraded, or denied.

## Readouts

| Readout | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Signal Quality | One of Good, Degraded, Acquiring, Mismatch, Clipped, No Lock or None. |
| Raw C/N, Effective C/N | Carrier-to-noise ratio before and after the ADC penalty. |
| Es/N0, C/N0 | Symbol energy to noise density, and carrier to noise density. |
| Required Es/N0 | What the chosen modulation and FEC need to lock. |
| Power Level, Noise Floor | Signal and noise power at the modem input. |
| ADC Level, ADC Status | How hard the converter is driven, and whether it is fine, clipping or starved. |
| Clip Penalty, Quant Penalty, Total Penalty | The C/N lost to ADC effects. |

The **Video Monitor** shows the received picture when the modem decodes a signal. It shows a glitch effect when the signal is degraded, NO SIGNAL when nothing matches, and stays blank when the modem is off.

## What a modem needs to lock

All of these must hold:

1. **Frequency.** The carrier's IF frequency falls inside the modem's bandwidth.
2. **Bandwidth.** The carrier fits inside the modem's bandwidth.
3. **Modulation and FEC.** Both match the transmitter.
4. **Margin.** Es/N0 exceeds the required value for that modulation and FEC.

The required Es/N0 follows the DVB-S2 table plus 1.0 dB of implementation loss. For example, QPSK 1/2 needs about 2.0 dB and QPSK 3/4 needs about 5.0 dB. BPSK needs about 3 dB less than QPSK at the same FEC. Higher-order modulation and higher FEC rates need a cleaner signal.

A modem locks when the margin is about 0.5 dB above the requirement and drops at 0.5 dB below it. A locked carrier with less than 1 dB of margin reads Degraded.

### Bandwidth clipping

If an upstream IF filter cuts into the carrier, the modem can still decode it when the FEC is strong enough. The minimum share of the carrier that must survive is:

| FEC | Minimum bandwidth kept |
|---|---|
| 1/2 | 40% |
| 2/3 | 50% |
| 3/4 | 60% |
| 5/6 | 75% |
| 7/8 | 85% |

Below the limit the modem reads Clipped.

### ADC effects

The ADC works best when the AGC holds the level near its target. A signal that is too strong drives the ADC into clipping. A signal that is too weak is lost in quantization noise. Both lower the Effective C/N. Check the AGC if ADC Status is not normal. See [Automatic Gain Control](/equipment/automatic-gain-control/).

### Interferers

An in-band interferer costs margin and the modem names it in its alarm line (for example "2 interferer(s) in band"). Remove it with the [Notch Filter](/equipment/notch-filter/) or a narrower [IF Filter Bank](/equipment/if-filter-bank/).

## Common tasks

### Tune a modem to a carrier

<Steps>

1. Open the **RX Analysis** tab and select a modem with **RX 1** to **RX 4**.
2. Find the carrier's IF frequency on the [spectrum analyzer](/equipment/real-time-spectrum-analyzer/).
3. Set **Frequency**, **Bandwidth**, **Modulation** and **FEC Rate** to match the carrier.
4. Press **Apply Changes**, and turn **Power** on if it is off.
5. Wait for Signal Quality to move from Acquiring to Good.

</Steps>

### Recover a degraded signal

<Steps>

1. Compare Es/N0 with Required Es/N0. The gap is your margin.
2. Check for interferers and notch them out.
3. Check the AGC and ADC Status.
4. Repoint the antenna or use a lower-order modulation if the margin is still short.

</Steps>

## Status and alarms

| Signal Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Good | Locked with margin. |
| Degraded | Locked with under 1 dB of margin, or an interferer or payload problem. |
| Acquiring | A carrier is present and the modem is still locking. |
| Mismatch | Modulation or FEC does not match the carrier. |
| Clipped | Upstream filtering has removed too much of the carrier. |
| No Lock | The carrier is below the threshold. |
| None | No carrier in the modem's bandwidth. |

The case alarm reads "Signal(s) Detected" when any powered modem is decoding a carrier.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing happens after editing a field | The change is staged. | Press Apply Changes. |
| Mismatch | Wrong modulation or FEC. | Match the transmitter. |
| No Lock with a strong carrier | Es/N0 below the requirement, or the bandwidth is too narrow. | Widen Bandwidth, fix the path, or lower the modulation order. |
| Clipped | The IF filter is narrower than the carrier. | Choose a wider IF filter. |
| Effective C/N far below Raw C/N | ADC clipping or starving. | Fix the level with the AGC. |
| The modem button is gray | The modem is off. | Turn Power on and wait about 4 seconds. |
