The Antenna Control Unit (ACU) manages the parabolic dish, the link between your ground station and a satellite. The dish collects the weak signal from space and focuses it on the feed. On transmit it concentrates your signal into a narrow beam toward the satellite. Gain of 40 to 60 dBi is common, so pointing matters: a dish that is a fraction of a degree off target loses signal fast.

![ACU Control tab with the polar plot showing the antenna position, the positioning controls and the tracking panel.](/images/shots/equipment/antenna-control-unit.webp)

## Where to find it

The ACU lives on the **ACU Control** tab of Mission Control. A station with more than one antenna has one tab per antenna, labeled with its band and size (for example "ACU: C-Band 9m"). See [ACU Control](/mission-control/acu-control/).

## Controls

| Control | What it does | Typical value |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Powers the ACU. With power off the dish cannot move. | On |
| Loopback | Routes the transmit signal back into the receive path for a self-test. | Off |
| STOW | Drives the dish to elevation 90 degrees at its current azimuth, the safe position. | n/a |
| MAINT | Drives the dish to elevation 5 degrees for access to the feed. | n/a |
| MANUAL | You set azimuth, elevation and polarization yourself. | n/a |
| PROGRAM | The dish follows the target satellite on its own. | n/a |
| Azimuth | Horizontal angle, clockwise from north. Adjustable in steps of 10, 1 and 0.01 degrees. Active in MANUAL mode only. | varies |
| Elevation | Angle above the horizon, 0 to 90 degrees, same step buttons. Active in MANUAL mode only. | varies |
| Polarization | Skew of the feed in degrees, same step buttons. | varies |
| APPLY / CANCEL | Commits or discards staged position changes. | n/a |
| Target Satellite | Picks the satellite to follow in PROGRAM mode. | n/a |
| Move to Target | Starts the slew to the chosen satellite. | n/a |
| Step-Track Optimization | Adds small corrections to PROGRAM tracking to peak the beacon. | Off for fast LEO satellites |

The ACU stages changes. The arrows change a staged value, and the dish does not move until you press **APPLY**. **CANCEL** drops the staged values. Changing the mode is also staged: the applied mode keeps running until APPLY.

### Step-track fields

These show only when Step-Track Optimization is on.

| Field | What it does | Typical value |
|---|---|---|
| Beacon Frequency (RF) | The satellite's beacon, in MHz. The line below it shows where the beacon lands on the IF. | varies |
| Search Bandwidth | Width of the beacon search in kHz (100 to 2000). | 500 kHz |
| Az Offset / El Offset | The corrections step-track has applied. | 0.000 degrees at start |
| Clear Offsets | Resets the corrections to zero. | n/a |

A warning reads "Not recommended for fast-moving satellites" when the target is a LEO satellite.

## Readouts

- **Mode** and **Pointing** (LOCKED or UNLOCKED) in the Tracking panel.
- **RF signals in view**: carriers inside the dish's main beam, whether or not locked.
- **Beacon C/N (1 kHz)** and **Beacon Lock** when a beacon is tracked.
- **OMT / Duplexer** card: TX and RX polarization, cross-pol isolation and status. See [Orthomode Transducer](/equipment/orthomode-transducer/).
- **Environmental** card: Feed Heater, Rain Blower, Precipitation and Ice Accumulation.
- **RF Metrics**: Beacon Freq, Gain, HPBW (beamwidth), G/T, Pol Loss and Sky Temp.

The **polar plot** shows azimuth as the angle around the circle (north at the top) and elevation as the distance from the center (zenith at the center, horizon at the edge).

## Common tasks

### Point at a geostationary satellite

<Steps>

1. Turn **Power** on and wait for the ACU to initialize.
2. Select **PROGRAM** and press **APPLY**.
3. Pick the **Target Satellite** and press **Move to Target**. The dish slews to the target.
4. Watch Pointing change to LOCKED and RF signals in view rise above zero.

</Steps>

### Point by hand

<Steps>

1. Select **MANUAL** and press **APPLY**.
2. Use the Azimuth and Elevation arrow buttons to stage the angles. Use the large arrows to get close and the small ones to peak.
3. Press **APPLY**.
4. Adjust Polarization to match the satellite until Pol Loss is small.

</Steps>

### Stow the dish

<Steps>

1. Select **STOW** and press **APPLY**.
2. The dish rises to 90 degrees elevation. Do this in high wind or before maintenance.

</Steps>

## Why pointing and polarization matter

- **Gain.** Gain grows with dish diameter and frequency. Doubling the diameter adds about 6 dB. More gain means a stronger received signal and a stronger transmit beam.
- **Beamwidth.** HPBW is roughly 70 times wavelength divided by diameter, in degrees. A larger dish has a narrower beam, so it must be pointed more precisely. Being one beamwidth off costs about 3 dB.
- **G/T.** The ratio of gain to system noise temperature. A higher value means the station hears weaker signals. A 9 m C-band dish with a good LNB reaches about 30 to 35 dB/K.
- **Polarization.** The feed must match the satellite's polarization. A skew error appears as Pol Loss in the RF Metrics.

## Status and alarms

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ANTENNA NOT OPERATIONAL | The antenna is faulted or out of service. |
| ACQUIRING LOCK... | The dish is moving or settling on a target. |
| LOCKED ON SATELLITE | Pointing is locked. |
| NO SIGNALS RECEIVED | The dish is not receiving any carrier. |
| AUTO TRACK FAILED | Step-track could not hold the beacon. |
| ACU AUTOMATION FAULT - MANUAL CONTROL ONLY | PROGRAM tracking is offline. Use MANUAL. |
| HIGH POLARIZATION | The feed skew is beyond 45 degrees. |
| RAIN FADE, ICE ON FEED | Weather is cutting the signal. Use the rain blower and feed heater. |
| SUN TRANSIT: ELEVATED SKY NOISE | The sun is near the beam and raises noise. |
| LOOPBACK ENABLED | Loopback is on. |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Arrows do nothing | The mode is not MANUAL, or power is off. | Select MANUAL and APPLY, or power on. |
| Dish does not move after arrow clicks | The change is staged. | Press APPLY. |
| UNLOCKED in PROGRAM mode | Target not selected, or the dish is still slewing. | Choose a target and Move to Target. |
| Signal weak with LOCKED pointing | Polarization is off. | Adjust Polarization and check Pol Loss. |
| Signal drops in rain | Rain fade or ice on the feed. | Check the Environmental card and run the blower and heater. |
