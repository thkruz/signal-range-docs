## Privileges Relied On

| Rule | Use here |
|------|----------|
| 47 CFR 97.301(e) | Technician privileges include 144-148 MHz |
| 47 CFR 97.305 | FM voice is an authorized emission on that band |
| 47 CFR 97.313(a) | Use the minimum power necessary for the contact |
| 47 CFR 97.119 | Identify with your call sign at the end of the communication and at least every ten minutes during it |

<Aside type="caution">
The exposure result on the station card applies to this installation only. Keep
people outside the area it specifies. Extra power cannot fix a wrong frequency
or make a busy channel acceptable.
</Aside>

## Operating Checklist

- [ ] Frequency and mode taken from the grant and band reference
- [ ] Battery, contacts, latch and antenna connection checked
- [ ] Channel monitored: no conversation in progress
- [ ] Simplex selected, low power set
- [ ] Call made with PTT (push-to-talk), then a pause for the reply
- [ ] Name, location and signal report exchanged
- [ ] Final identification given, PTT released

## End of Briefing

Riley will not key the radio for you. Make the call, leave room for the reply,
and sign off properly.
