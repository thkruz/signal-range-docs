| Spectrum span | 8 MHz | The whole hump has to fit on screen |

| Modem channel | 2.5 MHz, BPSK 1/2 | Deliberately mismatched — see below |

<Aside type="caution">
This receiver will **never** say LOCKED on GPS, and that is not a fault. A
spread-spectrum signal is smeared across two megahertz by a code your
demodulator does not have. Without the code, the signal genuinely *is* noise —
a broad, low hump lifting the floor rather than a stripe standing above it.
Detection and demodulation are different privileges.
</Aside>

## Timing on This Station

The rig's reference is GPS-disciplined. The console's **SOURCE** section
reports it:

| Readout | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| `REF` | Clock source — `GPS · n SATS`, `HOLDOVER`, or `ACQUIRING` |
| `CLK ΔT` | Accumulated timing offset, microseconds |
| `HOLD` / `GPS` button | Switches the reference between disciplined and free-running |

**Holdover** means the oven-controlled oscillator free-runs on its own
stability and ignores GPS entirely. It drifts slowly — but it drifts
*honestly*, which tonight may be the more valuable property.

## Failure Signatures Worth Knowing

| Symptom | Satellite count | Timing offset | Diagnosis |
|---------|----------------|---------------|-----------|
| Antenna disconnected | Drops to 0 | Freezes, then drifts | Outage |
| Sky obstructed | Drops | Degrades | Obstruction |
| **Healthy count, offset walking steadily** | **Unchanged** | **Growing** | **Something is lying** |

A real GPS failure takes satellites *away* from you. It is loud and obvious.
The dangerous case is the one where every indicator stays green and the
numbers quietly stop being true.

## End of Briefing

Everything with a clock in it trusts GPS: the power grid, financial
timestamps, cell networks, and the little oscillator in this radio. It's a
timing signal broadcast from twenty thousand kilometers up, at less power than
a lightbulb, with **no authentication of any kind**.

Nothing in the signal proves who sent it. The only things a liar can't fake
are the physics: how strong it should be, how wide, and how it should move.

Watch the numbers tonight.
