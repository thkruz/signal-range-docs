<Aside type="caution">
Keep test transmissions short; do not transmit into the fault for long. Power
the radio down before moving a load or a cable. Check coax continuity and
center-to-shield isolation only with both ends disconnected. Antenna DC
continuity depends on the antenna design and is a separate check.
</Aside>

| Rule | Use here |

| 47 CFR 97.301(e) | Technician privileges include 144-148 MHz |
| 47 CFR 97.305 | FM voice is authorized on the test channel |
| 47 CFR 97.119 | Identify during tests that radiate, including final contact |

A transmission into a dummy load does not radiate and needs no identification.
Tests through the antenna do.

## Troubleshooting Checklist

- [ ] Forward power, reflected power and SWR recorded briefly as the baseline
- [ ] Radio tested alone into the known-good load
- [ ] Load moved to the far end of the long cable and tested again
- [ ] Cable inspected along its route
- [ ] Continuity and isolation measured with power off, both ends open
- [ ] Repair made, cable routed without pinching, outdoor connections protected
- [ ] Both tests repeated under the original conditions, then a two-way report
