## Privileges Relied On

| Rule | Use here |
|------|----------|
| 47 CFR 97.301(e) | Technician HF privileges include 28.300-28.500 MHz |
| 47 CFR 97.305 | Phone is authorized on 28.300-28.500 MHz for this license class; data and CW have their own segments on the chart |
| 47 CFR 97.313(i) | Power limit for Technician operation on this band |
| 47 CFR 97.119 | Identification every ten minutes and at the end |

<Aside type="caution">
Keep the whole occupied bandwidth inside the segment, not just the dial
frequency. Extra power does not create a path that the ionosphere does not
offer.
</Aside>

## Operating Checklist

- [ ] Segment limits checked on the privilege card
- [ ] Mode and receive filter chosen for voice; compared with FM and CW examples
- [ ] Fine tuning and RIT used; transmit frequency confirmed unchanged by RIT
- [ ] Propagation examples reviewed: line of sight, ionospheric path, sporadic E, ducting, radio horizon
- [ ] Listened, called, microphone gain clean, report exchanged
- [ ] CW and an FT8 example inspected and placed on the band chart
- [ ] Any setting that crosses a band edge rejected
