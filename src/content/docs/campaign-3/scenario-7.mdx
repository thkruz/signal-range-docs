## Operating-Point Fixtures

These are authored training values for this radio, not specifications for a
commercial model and not the starter handheld.

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Receive current | 1 A |
| Transmit current | 8 A |
| Supply setting | 13.8 V |
| Radio reset threshold | 11.5 V at the radio's input |
| Portable battery (card) | 10 Ah usable |
| Portable duty cycle | 20% transmit, 80% receive |

The station worked before the lead was replaced during the last move. DC input
power is greater than the RF output because the radio is not perfectly
efficient.

<Aside type="caution">
Remove power before inspecting or replacing the lead. Do not bypass or enlarge
the fuse. Keep test transmissions into the dummy load short.
</Aside>

## Diagnosis Checklist

- [ ] Reset reproduced with the load; voltage and current captured before shutdown
- [ ] Voltage read at the supply and at the radio, at receive and at transmit
- [ ] Missing voltage worked out from current and lead resistance
- [ ] Power removed, connection inspected, lead replaced, fuse retained
- [ ] Transmit retested under load, no reset, then a contact made
- [ ] Portable endurance worked out from the battery card, with reserve allowed

---

## End of Briefing

A battery or supply that looks fine unloaded may sag under the radio. Compare
the two readings.
