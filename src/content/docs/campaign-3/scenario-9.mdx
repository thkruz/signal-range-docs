<Aside type="caution">
Computer notification sounds go out the same output device as the packet audio.
Silence them before transmit. Audio drive that is too high distorts the packet
and defeats decoding. Encoding a message is not secrecy, and an Internet
connection does not grant transmit privileges.
</Aside>
