## Investigation Checklist

- [ ] Noise pattern saved at fixed settings
- [ ] Battery baseline compared with the plugged-in station
- [ ] Accessories reconnected one at a time
- [ ] Short bearing exercise completed and limits explained
- [ ] Spare supply tried with identical receiver settings
- [ ] Two test transmissions compared: audio that follows mic gain at any power, and hum and squeal that follows power
- [ ] Remedy matched to the cause (lower mic gain versus a choke on the feed line), then confirmed with Riley
