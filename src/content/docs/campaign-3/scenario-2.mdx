## Link Reference

| Signal | Frequency | Bandwidth | Mode |
|--------|-----------|-----------|------|
| CUBEHOP-1 FM downlink | 435.250 MHz | 15 kHz | QPSK 1/2 |

### The Numbers That Matter

| Quantity | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Doppler excursion across the pass | **±10 kHz** |
| Receiver channel width | **30 kHz** |
| Tuning steps available | ±1 kHz (fine), ±10 kHz (coarse) |

Twenty kilohertz of total travel. The carrier starts about **10 kHz high**
while the satellite is approaching, slides down through the nominal frequency
at closest approach, and finishes about **10 kHz low** on the way out.

<Aside type="caution">
The drift is not linear. It is slowest at the very top of the pass — at
closest approach the range rate passes through zero, so for a few seconds the
frequency barely moves at all — and fastest near the horizon at both ends.
Expect to work hardest right after AOS and right before LOS.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note">
Set the tracking mode to **program-track first**, then pick the satellite.
Changing the mode clears the selected target — do it the other way round and
the rotator sits there doing nothing while your pass goes by.
</Aside>

## The Console's Offset Readout

The DECODER section shows **OFFSET** in hertz: how far the carrier sits from
the center of your channel. Positive means the carrier is above your VFO —
tune up. Negative means tune down.

That readout is exactly what the checkbox you're not allowed to touch reads.
Tonight you are the loop.

---

## Working the Pass

- [ ] Rotator in **program-track**, target CUBEHOP-1, before AOS
- [ ] Signal on the waterfall at 435.250 — expect it high of nominal at first
- [ ] Lock the downlink
- [ ] **Hold that lock for two solid minutes**, retuning as the carrier walks
- [ ] AFC stays **off** the entire time

## End of Briefing

Every satellite operator alive has, at some point, sat with one hand on a
tuning knob following a signal down a waterfall because there was nothing
clever available to do it for them.

It is the least efficient way to work a pass and the single best way to
understand what Doppler actually is. Two minutes. Then you get the checkbox —
in a couple of sessions.

Bird's up in four minutes. Go.
