## Board Fixtures

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Supply | 12.0 V DC |
| R1 | 120 ohm, 2 W |
| Indicator | LED with series resistor, polarity marked on the board |
| Fuse | Removable, rated, in a holder |
| Test points | SUP+ and SUP− (supply terminals), F1 IN / F1 OUT, SW1 IN / SW1 OUT, R1 A / R1 B, Q1 OUT, LED A / LED K, GND. Ammeter break points: SUPPLY FEED, R1 BRANCH, LED BRANCH |

<Aside type="caution">
Voltage is measured in parallel across a part, current in series with it. The
meter refuses a current-mode connection across the supply. Switch the supply
off and confirm it is off before any continuity test. Never measure resistance
on a live circuit.
</Aside>

---

## Safety and Scope

No capacitor on this board holds a charge. Stored-charge hazards are covered on
the reference card, not on the bench. Opening a mains-powered supply is not
part of this campaign.

---

## Bench Checklist

- [ ] Symbols on the drawing matched to parts on the board
- [ ] Meter function and sockets chosen before the probes touch anything
- [ ] Supply voltage read in parallel; any negative display explained
- [ ] R1 current and power worked out from the fixtures, then measured in series
- [ ] Power off and verified before continuity checks
- [ ] Repair followed by the same measurement at the same test points

---

## End of Briefing

Your calculation and the meter should agree. When they do not, one of them has
a wrong input. Find which.
