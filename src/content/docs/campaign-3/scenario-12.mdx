## Operations Checklist

- [ ] Site, cable route, public clearance and weather plan chosen
- [ ] Energy demand and reserve worked out from the actual kit
- [ ] Power and signal paths built and inspected; load test and receive check done
- [ ] Checked into the net with proper identification
- [ ] Any fault diagnosed from two relevant readings before parts are swapped
- [ ] Repair verified under the same condition that exposed the fault
- [ ] Moved to the fallback channel and reachability confirmed
- [ ] Message relayed exactly, phonetics for unusual words, fills requested, readback received
- [ ] Station identified, powered down, packed, reserve recorded, defects reported
