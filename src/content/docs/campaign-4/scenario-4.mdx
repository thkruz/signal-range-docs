## Transient or Trend

Two kinds of thing move on a telemetry panel, and the whole shift is telling them apart.

| | Transient | Trend |
|--|-----------|-------|
| What it looks like | A channel departs and returns on its own | A channel departs and keeps going |
| Cause | Usually none visible; the spacecraft absorbed something | Usually visible in the channel next to it |
| Rate to harm | Never gets there | Reaches a limit inside the window |
| What you do | Log the time and the peak; it becomes a point in a long-term trend | Name it, call it, command it, verify it |

Tonight's transient: reaction wheel 1 to ~5200 rpm for a minute at T+4:00 - a momentum bump, unloaded on its own. Tonight's trend: battery temperature climbing about three degrees a minute from T+7:00 with **heater state ON** beside it, forty minutes after eclipse exit. At that rate the battery is at the 40 °C red limit inside three minutes of the yellow crossing.

<Aside type="caution">
A state-of-health call that names the channel is a call somebody can act on. **Degraded - battery temperature in yellow and climbing, heater stuck ON, commanding TCS-HTR-OFF** is a call. **One channel in yellow is not a degraded bird** is not; nor is putting the payload to safe mode to protect a battery that a heater command protects just as well.
</Aside>

## Command, Then Verify

| Step | Where | What you see |
|------|-------|--------------|
| HPA up, modem 1 keyed | TX Chain | Chain ready inside the window |
| TCS-HTR-OFF | TT&C console | **ACK** - receipt, not execution |
| Heater state | Telemetry tab | OFF on the next frame - the command executed |
| Battery temperature | Telemetry tab | Turns over and comes back inside limits over the next few minutes - the command fixed what you sent it for |

A command is verified twice: the direct effect (the state it was meant to change) and the reason you sent it (the trend it was meant to stop). Log both times. Then chain cold before the log entry.

The heater still needs an engineer. That is the anomaly report to RAMPART, not the contact log. The log carries what the panel said and when.
</Aside>
