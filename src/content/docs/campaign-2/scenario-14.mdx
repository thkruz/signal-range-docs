### What each does to a Ku link

| Loss | Where it is | Fix | Number for today |
|------|-------------|-----|------------------|
| Rain attenuation | In the air, along the whole slant path | None on the rack. Reallocate, or ride through on margin | 30 mm/h at 11.7 GHz on a 19° pass: about **7 dB** (ITU-R P.838, 2.5 km rain height), plus a warmer sky |
| Ice on the feed | On the horn | **Feed heater**, before it forms | Severe sleet builds to 10 dB with a 12 min time constant; melts at 1 dB/min once the heater is on |

Rain attenuation rises steeply with frequency: a tenth of a decibel at C-band becomes whole decibels at Ku. A low pass makes it worse twice: a longer path through the rain, and less clear-sky margin to spend. The heater does nothing for rain.
