| Receive chain | RX Analysis | LNB 13,100 MHz LO, reference locked, 60 K, thermally stable | Retune modem 1 from 1,370 to **1,414 MHz**; analyzer centre **1,389 MHz**, span **2 MHz** | Read the LNB. **Leave modem 1 on 1,414**; it is Fiona's SAR-1 carrier until 06:29 |
| Transmit chain | TX Chain | BUC on external reference, current normal, under 70 °C; HPA output disabled | Confirm cold: powered, locked, disabled | Confirm cold |

<Aside type="caution">
Cold is not off. An HPA enabled with nothing driving it amplifies the noise floor into the feed, and a chain that was powered down needs a reference lock and a warm-up before it can be trusted. A receive-only shift leaves the transmit chain powered, on reference, and disabled.
</Aside>

## Link Parameters

LNB high-side at both sites: **IF = LO − RF** (13,100 MHz LO).

| Signal | RF (MHz) | IF (MHz) | Format |
|--------|----------|----------|--------|
| SAR-1 imagery downlink | 11,686 | 1,414 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |
| SAR-1 telemetry beacon | 11,711 | 1,389 | CW |
| SAR-2 imagery downlink | 11,730 | 1,370 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |

All passes are receive-only. The delivery standard is 8 dB or better held with the modem locked on a Galway-class pass; Shetland's lower SAR-2 pass is held at 7 dB. Digital links are a cliff, not a slope: above threshold the frames are the frames.
