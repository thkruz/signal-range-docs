## Uplink Plan

BUC is low-side injection: **RF = IF + LO** (12,600 MHz LO). The receive side is unchanged from acceptance.

| Path | IF (MHz) | LO (MHz) | RF (MHz) | Format |
|------|----------|----------|----------|--------|
| SAR-1 telecommand uplink | 1,405 | 12,600 (BUC) | 14,005 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz, −7 dBm modem |
| SAR-1 telemetry beacon (down) | 1,389 | 13,100 (LNB) | 11,711 | CW |
| SAR-1 imagery downlink | 1,414 | 13,100 (LNB) | 11,686 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |

### Doppler Across the Pass

| Link | Frequency | Peak Doppler | Handling |
|------|-----------|--------------|----------|
| Downlink | 11.7 GHz | ±270 kHz | Receiver AFC follows it |
| Uplink | 14.0 GHz | ±325 kHz | Pre-compensated at the console; the spacecraft cannot chase it |

The range rate at AOS is closing at roughly 7 km/s, passes through zero at culmination, and opens at the same rate on the way out. Uplink compensation is a console switch on the TT&C tab; it slews the command carrier against the predicted rate so the spacecraft sees 14,005 MHz throughout.

<Aside type="danger">
Order of operations on this chain is a safety interlock you enforce by hand: modem on air first, then HPA output. Enabling the HPA with nothing driving the BUC amplifies the BUC's own noise floor into the feed and fails the shift. Coming down, it mirrors: HPA off before the carrier stops.
</Aside>
