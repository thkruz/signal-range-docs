<Aside type="caution">
The constraint is the pedestal, not the spectrum. Two MERIDIAN downlinks do not interfere with each other and the scheduler does not time-share an antenna. Overlapping windows on one site are a conflict; the console will say so and refuse to validate until they are split.
</Aside>

### Why Shetland sees a different pass

SAR-1 is southbound on this orbit with its ground track west of Ireland. Shetland is further north, so the bird crosses its horizon two minutes before Galway's; Shetland is also further east of the track, so it never climbs as high (24° against 28°). Same bird, same element set, different place to stand. A sun-synchronous orbit at 97° inclination runs nearly pole to pole, and a site at 60° N sees more of its passes per day than one at 53° N. SAR-2 goes 86° overhead at Shetland fifteen minutes later; over Galway it peaks at 25°.

## Link Parameters

LNB is high-side injection at both sites: **IF = LO − RF** (13,100 MHz LO).

| Signal | RF (MHz) | IF (MHz) | Format |
|--------|----------|----------|--------|
| SAR-1 imagery downlink | 11,686 | 1,414 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |
| SAR-1 telemetry beacon | 11,711 | 1,389 | CW |
| SAR-2 imagery downlink | 11,730 | 1,370 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |
| SAR-2 telemetry beacon | 11,703 | 1,397 | CW |

### Expected numbers

| Pass | Decode window (C/N ≥ 8 dB) | Peak C/N |
|------|----------------------------|----------|
| SAR-1 from Shetland, 24° | about 14:03:30 to 14:06:00 | ~9.8 dB at 14:04:42 |
| SAR-1 from Galway, 28° (Charlie) | about 14:05:00 to 14:08:30 | ~11 dB at 14:06:45 |
| SAR-2 from Galway, 25° | about 14:20:30 to 14:24:00 | ~10 dB at 14:22:10 |

Shetland's peak is about a decibel under Galway's on the same bird: 20 log(860 / 761) is 1.1 dB of extra path loss, plus a little more atmosphere at the lower elevation. Every dB of difference between two sites should be explained by range and elevation. One that is not is a fault.

## End of Briefing

A second site is capacity, not a spare. With one antenna, one of every overlapping pair was going to be dropped; with two and a valid plan, all four get worked. And a second site is only capacity if somebody can fly it: today that somebody is you, from a console three hundred miles from the dish.

Fiona is waiting on the plan and the weather up there is not improving. Give her something she can fly, then fly hers.

Good luck.
