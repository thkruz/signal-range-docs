## The Shetland BUC

| Reading | Meaning | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| BUC over-temperature, fan alarm, TX modem 1 on air into the BUC, HPA off | The morning uplink check left a carrier driving the BUC. A BUC dissipates heat in proportion to its output; with the fan gone it settles above the 70 °C alarm as long as a carrier is going in | Carrier off, BUC **muted**, BUC **powered**. It settles well under 70 °C inside a couple of minutes |

<Aside type="caution">
Do not power the BUC off. Twice wrong: an unpowered BUC cools five times slower because its own regulation stops, and the alarm clears on a powered reading - an off BUC never reads normal, it reads off. Mute removes the heat source; power-off removes the thermometer.
</Aside>

Shetland's contact today is receive-only. The BUC is the transmit chain and plays no part in it; the receive path is healthy. The alarm gates nothing, and it is still a ticket for the fan and a note for whoever left the carrier up.

## The Reference

GW-01's GPSDO has been clean all week. If it is not clean during the window, read the panel before doing anything to it.

| Panel reads | It is | Do |
|-------------|-------|----|
| HOLDOVER active, GNSS switch **up**, zero satellites | A GNSS outage. The oscillator holds the reference to microseconds for tens of minutes; the uplink Doppler pre-compensation does not care | Fly. Leave the switch alone. Confirm re-lock when the signal returns |
| HOLDOVER active, GNSS switch **down** | An operator error | Switch up |
| Anything, after a power cycle | Ten minutes of warm-up | Do not. The window is gone |

Cycling the switch cannot conjure a satellite. A command goes up on a reference in holdover.
