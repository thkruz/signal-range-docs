### Two keys, two custodians

| Key | Direction | Rotates on | Moved by | Status reads on |
|-----|-----------|------------|----------|-----------------|
| Command key (KEY-2027-Q1 → Q2) | Up: authenticates what is sent | TT&C console, Begin then Complete | You, in the gap | TX chain (TX key) |

<Aside type="caution">
Between Begin and Complete the console reads **Pending Rotation**. A command sent in that state is rejected as key-invalid and written into the command history with that reason. A rotation in progress is not a key; the interval between the two buttons is the interval in which the fleet has no key at all. Send nothing in it.
</Aside>

## The Proof Pass, in Order

| Step | Action | Evidence |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Program-track MERIDIAN-SAR-2; beacon on RX analysis; chain cold | Beacon at 1,397 MHz IF |
| 2 | Lock 1,370 MHz, C/N ≥ 8 dB, payload decrypting | **Traffic key proven** under Q2. An uplink on this rack spoils the receive measurement, which is why the decode comes first |
| 3 | Uplink Doppler compensation; carrier into the muted BUC; unmute; HPA | Chain up in order, two minutes |
| 4 | KEY-VERIFY | ACK inside the window: **command key proven** under Q2 |
| 5 | HPA off, BUC muted, carrier off | Chain down in mirror order |

An ACK proves one thing on one bird. The decode proves a different key. Neither alone is the rotation; the record is a chain: order, retire, rotate, follow, prove.
