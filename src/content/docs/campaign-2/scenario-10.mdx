## Link Parameters

LNB high-side: **IF = LO − RF** (13,100 MHz). BUC low-side: **RF = IF + LO** (12,600 MHz).

| Signal | IF (MHz) | RF (MHz) | Format |
|--------|----------|----------|--------|
| SAR-1 telecommand uplink | 1,405 | 14,005 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz, −7 dBm modem |
| SAR-1 telemetry beacon | 1,389 | 11,711 | CW; visible from the horizon |
| SAR-1 imagery downlink | 1,414 | 11,686 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |

Doppler: up to ±270 kHz on the downlink (receiver follows), up to ±325 kHz on the uplink (compensated at the TT&C console, required for an ACK).
