## Uplink EIRP Plan

Build it from the modem outward. The HPA output is its input plus gain minus back-off, so the number at the end of the chain is only right if every stage before it is.

| Step | Item | Now | For this pass |
|------|------|-----|---------------|
| 1 | BUC gain | **18 dB** (maintenance run) | **23 dB**, the station standard. With 18 dB, a 4 dB back-off delivers the EIRP of a 9 dB one, with every indicator green |
| 1 | BUC compression | Output well under the 15 dBm P1dB | Stays out of compression at 23 dB |
| 2 | HPA back-off | 10 dB | **4 dB** (6 dB more EIRP). Below 3 dB the amplifier is overdriven and the IMD alarm trips; IMD worsens about 2 dB per dB of back-off given up |
| 3 | HPA output, amplifier up | Disabled | At least **0.5 W** into the feed (about 1 W expected at 4 dB on this chain); read on the TX chain power meter once the modem is on air and the HPA is enabled |
| After LOS | HPA back-off | 4 dB | **Back to 10 dB.** 4 dB was the exception for one geometry; on a normal pass it is 6 dB over the coordinated level and an interference risk |

An amplifier driven past its linear region makes new frequencies out of the ones it was given; they go up the same dish at the same EIRP into channels that belong to somebody else.

## Link Parameters

LNB high-side: **IF = LO − RF** (13,100 MHz). BUC low-side: **RF = IF + LO** (12,600 MHz).

| Signal | IF (MHz) | RF (MHz) | Format |
|--------|----------|----------|--------|
| SAR-1 telecommand uplink | **1,405** | 14,005 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz, −7 dBm modem. TX modem 1 is on 1,435 (SAR-2) and must be retuned |
| SAR-1 telemetry beacon | 1,389 | 11,711 | CW; visible from the horizon |
| SAR-1 imagery downlink | 1,414 | 11,686 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |

Doppler: up to ±270 kHz on the downlink (receiver follows), up to ±325 kHz on the uplink (compensated at the TT&C console, required for an ACK; engage it before AOS, not at it).
