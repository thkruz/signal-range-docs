## Acceptance Test Card GW-01-ATP-002

**Test:** MERIDIAN-SAR-1 imagery downlink, C/N at maximum elevation
**Method:** Predict from link budget; measure on the live receiver at three points on the pass; accept if the culmination measurement clears the demodulator threshold with margin and the shoulder readings follow the geometry.

### Site Survey Inputs (as loaded on the Link Analysis console)

| Parameter | Value | Source | Verifiable on the rack? |
|-----------|-------|--------|--------------------------|
| Satellite EIRP | 28 dBm | MERIDIAN payload data sheet | No |
| Slant range at max elevation | 761 km | Pass geometry, 28° culmination | Pass Schedule tab |
| Free-space path loss at 11,686 MHz | 171.4 dB | From the range above | Arithmetic |
| GW-01 receive antenna gain | 51.8 dBi | 4 m Ku, measured at feed | No |
| LNB gain | 65 dB | Survey; **read it on RX Analysis** | Yes |
| System noise temperature | 88 K | LNB 60 K plus sky, atmosphere and feed at 28° | No (understand it) |
| Occupied bandwidth | 36 MHz | QPSK 3/4 imagery carrier | Yes (modem) |
| Implementation loss | 1 dB | Pointing, polarization, modem | No |

Reference: C/N = EIRP + G<sub>rx</sub> − FSPL − L<sub>impl</sub> − 10 log<sub>10</sub>(k · T<sub>sys</sub> · B), with k = −228.6 dBW/K/Hz. Mind the EIRP unit; the survey quotes dBm.

Path loss scales with range squared: 20 log<sub>10</sub>(range ratio). At the 5° mask the slant range is 2,270 km, three times the culmination range, and FSPL is **180.9 dB**, 9.5 dB worse. That is the whole reason the measurement window is the middle of the pass.

### Pass Criteria

| Check | Criterion |
|-------|-----------|
| Predicted C/N | Within 1.0 dB of the design figure on the card (~11 dB) |
| Demodulator threshold | 6.0 dB (QPSK 3/4) |
| Required margin at acceptance | 2.0 dB over threshold, measured live at culmination |
| Shoulder readings | C/N at ~15° elevation on the rising and setting legs, about 7 dB each; the two must agree |
| Measurement point | On boresight under program-track, 14:06:45, 28°, 761 km |

<Aside type="caution">
The Commit Link button reads the active RX modem's live C/N at the moment you press it. Press it on the shoulder of the pass and you will accept a number that does not represent the station. The useful window is roughly 14:05:10 to 14:08:20.
</Aside>

## End of Briefing

A measurement that matches a prediction is evidence the station is performing as designed. A good measurement from a broken model is luck, and Rotterdam does not schedule tasking on luck.

Two shoulder readings that agree prove the curve. One culmination reading that matches the model proves the station. Charlie will countersign whatever you put on the card. Make it a number you can defend.

Good luck.
