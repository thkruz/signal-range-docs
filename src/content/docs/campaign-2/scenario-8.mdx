## Link Parameters

LNB high-side: **IF = LO − RF** (13,100 MHz). BUC low-side: **RF = IF + LO** (12,600 MHz).

| Signal | IF (MHz) | RF (MHz) | Format |
|--------|----------|----------|--------|
| SAR-1 imagery downlink | 1,414 | 11,686 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |
| SAR-1 telemetry beacon | 1,389 | 11,711 | CW |
| SAR-1 telecommand uplink | 1,405 | 14,005 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz, −7 dBm modem |
| SAR-2 imagery downlink | 1,370 | 11,730 | Not worked from Galway tonight |

Doppler: ±270 kHz on the downlink (receiver follows), ±325 kHz on the uplink (compensated at the TT&C console, required for an ACK). A 40 degree pass has a higher peak range rate than the afternoon passes; the compensation handles it, provided it is on.
