## SAR-3 Link Parameters

LNB high-side: **IF = LO − RF** (13,100 MHz). BUC low-side: **RF = IF + LO** (12,600 MHz).

| Signal | IF (MHz) | RF (MHz) | Format |
|--------|----------|----------|--------|
| SAR-3 telemetry beacon | 1,315 | 11,785 | CW |
| SAR-3 imagery downlink (test pattern) | 1,340 | 11,760 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz |
| SAR-3 TT&C uplink | 1,465 | 14,065 | QPSK 3/4, 36 MHz, −7 dBm modem |
| SAR-1 imagery downlink (for contrast) | 1,414 | 11,686 | What the RX modem is on now |

Doppler: up to ±270 kHz on the downlink, up to ±325 kHz on the uplink; compensation at the TT&C console is required for an ACK.

<Aside type="caution">
Secure the uplink before you decode. SAR-3 turns the station's own 14,065 MHz
command carrier around through its command transponder at 11,810 MHz, co-polar
with the receive chain and inside its band. With the amplifier up that return
arrives some 50 dB above the test pattern, the AGC pulls the whole chain down
and the video reads a few decibels of C/N instead of eleven. Both ACKs are in
hand by then; the amplifier has nothing left to do until the next command.
</Aside>
