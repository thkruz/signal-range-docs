## The Window

Two commands inside 13:20:21-13:29:09, in order: **HK-DUMP**, then **PLD-STATUS**. Chain up before the first (carrier into the muted BUC, unmute, HPA), chain down at LOS in the mirror order.

The console gives four reasons for a NAK. Three of them are its own:

| NAK reason | What to check | Fix |
|------------|---------------|-----|
| Uplink not compensated | TT&C console Doppler toggle | Engage it |
| COMSEC key invalid | TX chain key badge | Rotate |
| Outside command window | Window badge | Wait |
| **Uplink denied - carrier jammed** | Everything above reads clean; the transponded return on the analyzer shows a carrier that is not yours | **Hop** |

<Aside type="caution">
A NAK with the key reading **Valid** at both ends, Doppler engaged and the window **OPEN** is not a station problem and not a key problem. It is the satellite failing to hear you because something louder is sitting on your uplink frequency at the bird. Rotating a good key in an open window spends the window on nothing. Standing down hands them the window.
</Aside>

## The Hop Set

Frequency hopping is only agile to someone who does not have the sequence. The key **is** the sequence. Both ends must load it - Anneke on SAR-2, you on the Security console - or one of you is transmitting into nothing.

| Step | Where | What you see |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Call the denial | The decision | Anneke keys SAR-2 on your call |
| Load Hop-Set Key | Security console, TRANSEC card | Key badge **LOADED** |
| Waveform mode → Frequency hopping | Same card | Sync badge **SYNC LOCKED** |
| Resend PLD-STATUS | TT&C console | **ACK received** - through the jamming |

What hopping changes: your carrier is spread over five channels a jammer parked on one cannot cover, and the bird follows because it has the sequence. What it does not change: the jammer is still there, the set is now spent, and the report still has to say what was seen.

<Aside type="note">
A hop set used on the air is a hop set observed. HOP-SAR2-04 is retired tonight and the next set is staged before the next commanding pass. You hop on a called denial - not from AOS. Hopping every pass burns a set for a denial that may not come.
</Aside>
