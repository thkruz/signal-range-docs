## Priya's Rule

| Step | What | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Look | Read the log as a set, read the pass as a whole | Three small warnings are one pattern; one carrier is a geometry |
| Flag | Acknowledge the entry on the Security console | The flag is the first line of the incident record |
| Note the time | The time *you saw it*, in the record | A change without a time behind it is a change nobody can explain at 09:00 |
| Act | Disable, retune, report | The action is what the record justifies - never the other way round |

<Aside type="caution">
Nothing tonight is loud. A spray is designed to look like three unrelated warnings. A ground emitter in your band looks like a bad pass. A configuration export on a decommissioned account looks like a service poll. The console gives you entries; the pattern is yours to find.
</Aside>

## The Carrier

From about 02:25:30, four minutes into the SAR-1 pass, a second carrier appears at 1,410 MHz IF (11,690 MHz RF) - a megahertz wide, 4 MHz below the video and inside its band. The video's C/N drops with it.

Two things to check before calling it:

- **Does it move?** Over a minute the SAR-1 video walks ~200 kHz down the span with the Doppler. Frame the carrier on a 2 MHz span and watch. A signal with no Doppler on a pass that has plenty is not on the bird.
- **Does it outlive the bird?** LOS is 02:29:34. A carrier that is still there afterwards was never coming from any satellite.

Fiona has SAR-1 clean from Shetland the whole pass. Whatever you are seeing is Galway's.

<Aside type="note">
This is the first **decision** in the campaign. It is graded on what the station actually shows - not on which option sounds right. Read the carrier and the key status before you answer: a call made without looking scores half.
</Aside>

## The Export

While you are writing up the carrier, the log moves: **02:33, a full station-configuration export by the legacy telemetry account** - antenna, LNB LO, modem plan, contact schedule. No change ticket. Thirty-five minutes after that account failed three logins.

A contact schedule is a list of where an antenna will point, when, on what frequency. Flag it, note the time, then disable the account - in that order.
